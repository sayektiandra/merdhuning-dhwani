# Attribution Guide

This document provides simple guidelines for attributing original works and adaptations published in this repository.

Works explicitly identified as licensed under **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)** may be used, shared, and adapted in accordance with the terms of that license.

## 1. Attribution of Original Works

When sharing or using a work without modifications, include the following information:

- **Title:** Title of the work.
- **Creator:** Name of the original creator.
- **License:** Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).
- **Source:** Link to the original work, if reasonably practicable.

Example:

Work: [Work Title]  
Creator: [Creator's Name]  
License: [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)  
Source: [Link to Original Work]

## 2. Attribution of Adapted Works

When translating, modifying, or adapting a work, retain attribution to the original creator and indicate the changes made.

Include the following information:

- **Original Work:** Title of the original work.
- **Original Creator:** Name of the original creator.
- **Source:** Link to the original work, if reasonably practicable.
- **Original Work License:** CC BY 4.0.
- **Changes:** Brief description of the modifications made.
- **Adapter:** Name of the party who created the adaptation.
- **Adapted Material License:** License established by the adapter for their own contributions and rights, if any.

Example:

Original Work: [Original Work Title]  
Original Creator: [Creator's Name]  
Source: [Link to Original Work]  
Original Work License: [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)  
Changes: [Translation into Indonesian / Lyric adaptation / Other modifications]  
Adapter: [Adapter's Name]  
Adapted Material License: [License established by the adapter, if any]

The license established by the adapter applies only to their own contributions and rights. It does not replace or restrict the public's permission to use the original work under CC BY 4.0.

## 3. Attribution in Live Performances

For live performances where no visual medium or space is available for displaying complete attribution, the following simplified format may be used:

**[Work Title] — by [Creator's Name], licensed under CC BY 4.0.**

Where practicable, information about the original source may be provided through a song list, event program, publication page, or other links.

## 4. General Requirements

- Retain copyright and license notices provided with the work.
- Indicate changes whenever the work is translated, modified, or adapted.
- Attribution to the original creator does not imply that the creator endorses or approves an adaptation made by another party.
- Adapters may establish separate licensing terms for their own contributions and rights, provided that such terms do not restrict the public's rights to use the original work under CC BY 4.0.
- Attribution formats may be adjusted to suit the medium and context of use, provided that the license requirements are fulfilled.
